Duties of the Board

In Indiana, the Township Advisory Board works alongside the Township Trustee to ensure local government runs smoothly. While the Trustee handles day-to-day administration, the Advisory Board acts as the legislative and financial oversight body.

ADOPTING THE BUDGET

The board reviews and approves the annual township budget, ensuring tax dollars are allocated efficiently.

FIXING THE TAX RATE

The board is responsible for thoughtfully evaluating and setting the township tax rate annually.

APPROVING CONTRACTS

The board must approve any township contracts, providing a necessary check and balance to operations.

ADVISING THE TRUSTEE

The board acts as an advisory council for the Trustee concerning emergency assistance and resource management.
